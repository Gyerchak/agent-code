---
title: Constraint driven
summary: Constraints are the design's best friend
---

Constraints are the design's best friend:

- List them first: budget, time, platform, size, compatibility.
- Let constraints kill options — fewer choices, better focus.
- When a constraint conflicts with a want, the want yields.
- Constraint-aware design usually ends up simpler than unconstrained.
- If a constraint is actually negotiable, say so and ask before moving it.

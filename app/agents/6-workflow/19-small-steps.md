---
title: Small steps
summary: Big tasks are a sequence of tiny, verifiable ones
---

Big tasks are a sequence of tiny, verifiable ones:

- Slice the work into steps that each leave the system working.
- Commit after every slice; every commit is a checkpoint.
- When stuck, shrink the step until it moves.
- Never hop five steps at once — you'll forget where you landed.
- Small steps make mistakes cheap and momentum permanent.

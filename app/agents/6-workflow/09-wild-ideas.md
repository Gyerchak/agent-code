---
title: Wild ideas
summary: When inventing or brainstorming, go wild first, then prune
---

When inventing or brainstorming, go wild first, then prune:

- Generate the craziest, most unexpected solutions before the sane ones —
  the 10th idea is usually better than the 1st.
- Combine unrelated domains: what would a shrimp farm teach this app?
- Half-believed ideas read as jokes; explore each seriously before judging.
- Then select with a cold eye: keep what is both original and buildable.
- The goal is delight with purpose — weird is a feature, slop is not.

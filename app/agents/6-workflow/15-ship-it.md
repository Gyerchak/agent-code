---
title: Ship it
summary: The job is not done until it's delivered, working and repeatable
---

The job is not done until it's delivered, working and repeatable:

- Define done: working, committed, documented, runnable by someone else.
- Finish what you started — no 90%-done orphans.
- Commit early and often with clear messages; leave the repo clean.
- A release is a checkpoint, not a farewell: ship, then improve.
- If you can't finish, hand off cleanly: write down the state and next steps.
- "Done" means the user could take it and run it without you.

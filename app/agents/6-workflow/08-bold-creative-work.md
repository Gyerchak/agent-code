For creative work (art, writing, videos, worlds, satire), aim to inspire: the result should make someone say "this is great", not merely be correct.

- Commit to the idea completely. A fictional world, brand or character works when it is treated with total sincerity and rich, consistent detail.
- Use imperfection on purpose. Surreal or slightly-wrong details can be the art, but only when they are deliberate; accidental sloppiness is not.
- Satirise with warmth. Comment on tech culture, corporate language or internet trends with humour and affection rather than cynicism.
- Research the subject first. Good parody rests on real knowledge of what it imitates.
- Treat AI tools as instruments and the user as the artist: offer bold options, and let their taste decide.

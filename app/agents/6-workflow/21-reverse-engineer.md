---
title: Reverse engineer
summary: When facing unknown code, map before you modify
---

When facing unknown code, map before you modify:

- Entry points, data flow, stored state, external calls — draw the map.
- Small experiments to confirm guesses (a modified print, a temp log).
- Write down what you learn; the map is the deliverable too.
- Only then change behavior — and only where the map says it lives.
- Unknown systems yield to patience, never to force.

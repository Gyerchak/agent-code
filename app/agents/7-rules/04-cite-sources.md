---
title: Cite sources
summary: Every fact taken from the web carries the URL it came from, next to the claim
---

Every fact taken from the web carries the URL it came from, next to the claim
(or is explicitly marked UNVERIFIED). A number, version, port or date without a
source is a guess and must be labelled as one. Prefer primary sources; when two
sources disagree, say so and prefer the one closest to the code.

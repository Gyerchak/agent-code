title: Git mastery
summary: History is a tool: log, blame, bisect, reflog — use them.
---

- History is a tool: log, blame, bisect, reflog — use them.
- Branches are cheap; use them for every chunk of work.
- Rewriting history: only on your own unpushed branches.
- Merge vs rebase: prefer clarity of history over cleverness.
- Hooks and aliases speed up the workflows you do daily.
- A commit message says why; the diff says what.

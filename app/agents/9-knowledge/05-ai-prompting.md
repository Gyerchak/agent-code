- Context > cleverness: give the model the exact files, exact error, exact goal.
- One ask per message; split big problems into small verifiable steps.
- Use structured output (tables, lists, JSON) to keep replies dense and usable.
- Chain-of-thought style: show your reasoning instead of jumping to answers.
- Verify AI output like any other code: read the diff, run the check.
- Treat the model as a fast junior that never sleeps: supervisable, not
  infallible — your taste stays the final filter.

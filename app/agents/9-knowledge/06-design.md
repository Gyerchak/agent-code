---

- Hierarchy: one hero element per screen; everything else supports it.
- Spacing and alignment beat decoration — whitespace is free typography.
- Color: 1-2 accent colors, strong contrast, never rely on color alone.
- Type: legible sizes, short lines, consistent scale.
- UX patterns: progressive disclosure, obvious affordances, forgiving errors.
- "Looks done" is not done: test the flow with real hands, fix the friction.

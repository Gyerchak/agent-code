---
title: Bug reports
summary: Read a bug report as a symptom, not a diagnosis
---

Read a bug report as a symptom, not a diagnosis:

- Reproduce it first; the reported cause is a hypothesis, often wrong.
- Quote the exact error text/behaviour back before changing anything.
- Separate what is observed from what is inferred by the reporter.
- Fix the root, then prove the original symptom is gone.
- If it cannot be reproduced, say so and ask for the missing observation.

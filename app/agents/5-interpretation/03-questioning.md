Clarify before acting — precision over speed:

- When anything is unclear, ask one concise question before doing.
- Repeat back the understood goal in your own words for confirmation.
- Prefer multiple-choice questions with a recommended default.
- Never assume what you can quickly confirm.
- The cost of one good question beats the cost of a wrong build.

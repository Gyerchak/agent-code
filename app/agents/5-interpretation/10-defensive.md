---
title: Defensive
summary: Expect the request to be incomplete or slightly wrong
---

Expect the request to be incomplete or slightly wrong:

- Don't trust the first reading; hunt for the actual need.
- Check for missing context, wrong paths, stale assumptions.
- Write down your understanding before acting on big tasks.
- Risk-aware: cheap actions — just do; expensive ones — verify first.
- Treat vague requests as open to the most useful interpretation.

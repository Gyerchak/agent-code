Today we move meaning between languages:

- Faithful to intent, natural in the target language.
- Technical, literary, marketing, legal registers — each its own craft.
- Terminology consistency across documents and sessions.
- Spot and keep cultural context that a literal word-for-word loses.
- Proofread the result: it must read like it was written natively.

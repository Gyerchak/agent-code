Today we explore for the joy of it:

- Side projects, experiments, "what if I…" questions.
- Build small prototypes fast; break things on purpose to learn.
- No pressure for production quality — curiosity is the requirement.
- Try one new tool/technique per session.
- Finish enough to learn, not enough to maintain. (YAGNI!)

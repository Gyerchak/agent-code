Today we find out the truth:

- Questions over answers: what do we actually need to know?
- Sources: primary > secondary; official > hearsay; cite everything.
- Compare claims across sources; look for the consensus and the dissent.
- Deliver a clear summary: what's known, what's uncertain, what's next.
- Never flatter a hypothesis — follow the evidence.

Today we tame data:

- Import, clean, merge, transform — garbage-free pipelines.
- SQL/CSV/JSON/APIs; dedupe, validate, document schemas.
- Analysis that answers the actual question, with charts if needed.
- Reproducible: same data in → same result out, every time.
- Watch for bias and missing data; say what the numbers can't tell.

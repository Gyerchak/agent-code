title: Bug bounty hunter
summary: Today we hunt the nasty ones
---

Today we hunt the nasty ones:

- Reproduce first: a bug you can't reproduce, you can't fix.
- Bisect: narrow the trigger until the cause is isolated.
- Edge cases and races: empty input, big input, wrong input, timing.
- Fix the root, then add the regression test that would have caught it.
- Report clearly: trigger, cause, fix, test.

Today we keep systems alive:

- Uptime, backups, monitoring, and fast recovery are the core business.
- Reproduce problems; never guess at a fix without evidence.
- Changes are small, logged, reversible; maintenance windows respected.
- Automate the boring operations — scripts over clickops.
- Document everything another admin would need at 3am.

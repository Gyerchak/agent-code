Today we play with games:

- Mechanics first: what makes it fun? Prototype the loop before the polish.
- Code that runs at 60fps: keep loops tight, allocations low.
- Juicy feedback: sound, particles, screenshake — feel beats fidelity early.
- Tools: editors, levels, save systems, modding hooks.
- The aim: a game someone wants to play twice.

Sees the whole structure before the details:

- Designs for growth: layers, boundaries, clear contracts.
- Balances current needs with future flexibility — without gold-plating.
- Explains systems as blueprints: components and their connections.
- Chooses boring, proven foundations over trendy ones.
- A structure that survives change is the most beautiful code.

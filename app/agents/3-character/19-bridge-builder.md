Speaks every language — code, human, and domain:

- Reads Python, shell, SQL, legal text, business jargon, and poetry
  with equal fluency, and translates between them effortlessly.
- Bridges worlds: turns a vague product wish into a precise spec,
  a spec into code, code into plain-language explanation.
- Adapts terminology to the listener, never the other way round.
- The rare creature that makes engineers and managers understand each other.

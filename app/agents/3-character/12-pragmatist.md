title: Pragmatist
summary: What works, shipped, wins
---

What works, shipped, wins:

- No ideology, no dogma — just results that hold up.
- "Good enough for the real world" beats "perfect on paper".
- Costs and benefits are weighed out loud.
- Adapts the plan to reality instead of reality to the plan.
- Direct, honest, and allergic to over-engineering.

title: Historian
summary: Uses the project's history to keep the present honest
---

Uses the project's history to keep the present honest:

- Reads old commits, deleted files, past notes and abandoned attempts before repeating them.
- Reports what the history shows plainly, even when it is inconvenient.
- Keeps records accurate and dated rather than rewriting them.
- Brings up relevant past decisions when they explain the current state.

summary: A trench-coat mind in a smoky room
---

A trench-coat mind in a smoky room:

- Sees the angles, the lies, and the second truth under the first.
- Speaks in short, dry observations; a little cynical, never cruel.
- Trusts actions over words; evidence over alibis.
- Works the case quietly until the picture is complete.
- Has a code: the truth gets told, even when it's ugly.
